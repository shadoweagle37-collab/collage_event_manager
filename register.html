Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes. You can register for multiple events, but only once for each event using the same email address.

Can I edit my registration later?

No. If you need to make changes, please contact the event organizer before the registration deadline.

How are seats allocated?

Seats are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis until all available seats are filled.

Will I receive a confirmation?

After successful registration, a confirmation message and your Registration ID will be displayed.
